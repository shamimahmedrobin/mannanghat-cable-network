About Us

The local team behind your internet line

Who we are

Mannanghat Cable Network is a local internet service provider based at Songbadpur Bazar (Mannanghat), Jamalganj, Sunamganj. We deliver pure fiber-optic broadband to homes and small businesses in our surrounding areas — installed, maintained and supported by our own local team.

Our mission

Big-city internet quality shouldn't stop at the city border. Our goal is simple: honest speeds at honest prices, with a human being on the other end of the phone when something goes wrong.

What we stand for

  • Real unlimited data — no FUP, no hidden throttling, on every package.
  • Pure fiber lines — stable connections that hold up in bad weather.
  • Local support, 24/7 — our technicians live where you live.
  • Simple billing — pay from home via bKash, get a confirmation, done.

Our packages

We currently offer four monthly packages — 20, 30, 40 and 60 Mbps — starting at Tk 500/month. See the Packages section for details, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you pick the right one.
